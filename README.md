# 🚀 FinRight AI  
### *Your Personal AI Finance Coach*  

FinRight AI is an intelligent financial advisor that helps users understand, manage, and improve their spending habits through AI-driven insights.  
Instead of just tracking expenses like traditional apps — FinRight AI analyzes uploaded CSV/PDF/receipt data and gives **personalized money-saving suggestions**, **top spending summaries**, and even answers questions like:

> **"Should I buy this right now?"**  
> *AI replies: Yes/No based on your spending pattern*

---

## 📌 Features

- 🔍 **Smart Expense Summary**
  - Detect top expenses, categories & overspending areas

- 📊 **Deep Spending Analytics**
  - Trend analysis, month-wise spending breakdown, high usage detection

- 💡 **Money-Saving Suggestions**
  - Personalized tips generated based on behavior to reduce unnecessary expenses

- 🧠 **Purchase Decision Engine**
  - Ask: *"Can I buy headphones right now?"*  
  - AI responds **YES/NO** based on budget + spending history

- 📄 **Multi-file Upload Support**
  - Upload CSV, PDF invoices, or even receipt images

- 🌍 **Multiple Language Support** *(Future Scope)*
  - Hindi | English | Marathi | + More

- 📱 **Cross-Platform Support**
  - Compatible with Android + iOS *(planned mobile app release)*

- 🧑‍💼 Easy to use for:
  - Students | Workers | Busy Professionals | General public

## 🏗 How It Works

1. Upload CSV/PDF/Receipt image  
2. AI reads & understands your expenses  
3. Generates spending summary & category breakdown  
4. Provides money saving guidance + yes/no buying decisions  
5. You get **clarity instead of confusion.**
